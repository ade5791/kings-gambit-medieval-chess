Anno Domini MCDXCII

King's Gambit

Cinematic 3D chess in a torch-lit hall. Play a computer opponent, share a board locally, or open an online hall - rendered in the browser with three.js, with the chess search running off the main thread.

Modes

Versus computer

Three difficulties. Easy plays a randomised legal move; medium searches to depth 3; hard searches to depth 5.

Two players

Hotseat on a single device.

Online

Host or join a hall by code through a relay server.

Showcase

A self-playing demo you can pause.

Controls

InputAction
Click / tap a pieceSelect it, then tap a highlighted square to move
DragOrbit the camera
ScrollZoom
FFlip the camera to the other side
TToggle the tactical overhead view
CToggle cinema mode (hides the HUD)
SpacePause / resume - showcase mode only
EscapeClose the settings panel

F, T, C and Space act only while a match is in play, and are ignored while typing in a field. Every binding above was read from the keydown handler in the shipped source, not assumed.

Quality

Four presets

Low, Medium, High and Ultra, selectable in Settings during play.

Touch surfaces

Portrait and landscape are gated at a 44px minimum touch target, verified against the deployed build.

Reduced motion

Decorative loops are cancelled under prefers-reduced-motion.

Built with three.js and chess.js. Chess search runs in a Web Worker.
